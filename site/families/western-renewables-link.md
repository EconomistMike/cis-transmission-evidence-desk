# Western Renewables Link

## Overview

WRL-PROJECT-WRL | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 6 sources | 30 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One continuous WRL Project, formerly named Western Victoria Transmission Network Project. The physical line, terminals, reactors, transformers and protection/control works are Components; the May 2023 uprate and North Ballarat terminal relocation is a scope Version. VNI West is a distinct related Project sharing the Bulgana terminal interface.

**Current status.** Anticipated project in the AEMO 2026 ISP; EES public hearing concluded 3 March 2026 with no archived outcome. Not committed, approved or funded on the archived record.

**Capacity.** AEMO: 1,460 MW of renewable capacity harnessed under the uprated scope (600 MW original design), 695 MW additional MEL-WNV network capacity and 310 MW REZ V2. AusNet separately claims capability of more than 3,000 MW.

**Schedule.** AEMO 2026 expects completion November 2029, superseding the 2024 mid-2027 / July-2027 representation. Not energisation or commercial operation.

**Owner.** AusNet is the archived proponent; Victorian transmission planning responsibility transferred to VicGrid on 1 November 2025. No ownership or delivery instrument is archived.

**Funding pathway.** Not established in the archive. No RIT-T PACR, NEVA order, regulatory determination or funding instrument was acquired.

**Delivery status.** Anticipated (AEMO 2026 classification); construction status not evidenced.

**Dependent projects.** VNI West (connects at a terminal station near Bulgana; WRL is an interface/prerequisite). South West Victoria Expansion is a separate future project with a WRL prerequisite. Western Victoria Reinforcement is a distinct VicGrid program, not a WRL component.

## Timeline

- 2019-07 — Western Victoria Renewable Integration RIT-T completed (decision (underlying record unavailable)): AEMO records that a RIT-T was completed in July 2019; the original design was 600 MW. The PACR itself was not acquired. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 24]
- 2021 — NCIPAP minor 220 kV augmentations completed (delivered (related works)): Minor line augmentations on Red Cliffs-Wemen-Kerang-Bendigo and Moorabool-Terang-Ballarat 220 kV lines completed through AusNet Services NCIPAP. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 24]
- 2023-05 — WRL scope and timing augmented via VNI West PACR and NEVA order (scope version change): North Ballarat terminal station relocated to Bulgana and the line from north of Ballarat to Bulgana uprated from 220 kV to 500 kV; capacity moves from 600 MW design to 1,460 MW. Underlying VNI West PACR remains unarchived; the NEVA order is now archived (VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S267, Special Gazette S267, 27 May 2023) - see the 2023-05-27 timeline entry. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 24]
- 2019-07-19 — WRL PACR published by AEMO (precise date) (decision (underlying record still unavailable)): The VNI West and WRL Ministerial Order defines 'WRL PACR' as 'the Western Victoria Renewable Integration Project Assessment Conclusion Report published by AEMO on 19 July 2019.' The PACR document itself remains unarchived; see SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S267; 2]
- 2023-05-27 — VNI West and WRL Ministerial Order made under s16Y of the Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005, published as Special Gazette S267 (legal instrument (NEVA order)): Order dated 26 May 2023, published 27 May 2023. Schedule 2 specifies the WRL Uprate augmentation: a new 500 kV double-circuit line from Sydenham Terminal Station to Bulgana Terminal Station with two new 500/220 kV 1,000 MVA transformers at a new Bulgana switchyard, plus works at Sydenham, Ballarat and Elaine Terminal Stations. The reasons state the WRL segment from Mount Prospect ('north of Ballarat') to Bulgana was to be uprated from 220 kV to 500 kV and the Mount Prospect terminal site relocated to Bulgana, enabling additional generation capacity in Western Victoria 'by as early as 2027'. This order is the legal source underlying AEMO's later 2024/2026 ISP scope summaries. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S267; 3, 8, 12-13]
- 2024-06 — AEMO 2024 ISP representation, project renamed (planner representation (superseded)): Recorded as Western Renewables Link (uprated), formerly named Western Victoria Transmission Network Project; anticipated, 1,460 MW, expected completion mid-2027 with July 2027 full capacity after testing. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; 19]
- 2025-11-01 — Victorian transmission planning responsibility transferred to VicGrid (governance change): Transmission network planning responsibilities transitioned from AEMO, through AEMO Victorian Planning, to VicGrid. This does not by itself establish WRL ownership. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 36]
- 2026-03-03 — WRL EES public hearing concluded (proponent-reported milestone): AusNet states the WRL Environment Effects Statement public hearing concluded on 3 March 2026; no outcome or approval is stated and no EES record is archived. [WRL-AUSNET-PROJECT; derivative line 75]
- 2026-06 — AEMO 2026 ISP representation (current planner evidence): Anticipated project, 500 kV double-circuit Bulgana to Sydenham, 1,460 MW harnessed, 695 MW additional MEL-WNV network capacity, V2 310 MW, expected completion November 2029, no reported cost. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 24-25]
- 2025-12-12 — AusNet appoints ACCIONA Genus Joint Venture as WRL construction contractor (proponent-reported milestone): AusNet announces appointment of a joint venture between ACCIONA and Genus to undertake WRL construction works, and describes the line as a proposed 190-kilometre 500 kV overhead line from near Bulgana to Sydenham. Construction can proceed if the project receives the necessary planning approvals; no cost figure is stated on the release. [WRL-AUSNET-2025-CONTRACTOR; web]
- 2026-09-03 — AusNet project webpage captured (proponent record): States a proposed 190 km Bulgana to Sydenham line and capability of more than 3,000 MW; gives no estimate and no completion date. [WRL-AUSNET-PROJECT; derivative lines 41, 49, 75]

## Warnings and open decisions

- SHARED_ATTRIBUTION#5 (blocker): Bulgana Terminal Station is a shared WRL / VNI West asset: AEMO 2026 states VNI West will connect at a terminal station near Bulgana to WRL, while the WRL delivery scope contains the new Bulgana 500 kV switchyard, the two 1,000 MVA 500/220 kV transformers and the second 220 kV bus. The transformer and hub cells rest on shared plant. No allocation between WRL and VNI West is made or inferable from the archive; do not attribute a share of Bulgana works, or their cost, to either project. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p25 (WRL scope); p51 (VNI West connects at Bulgana)]
- OUTTURN_UNKNOWN#11 (blocker): No WRL cost estimate, regulatory allowance, contract or outturn of any date is archived; both AEMO project tables leave the WRL cost column empty. The chart's cost and millions-per-km cells cannot be published as sourced figures as they stand. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p19; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp24-25]

- CONTRADICTION#8 (warning): AEMO 2024 states expected completion mid-2027 (July 2027 for full capacity after testing); AEMO 2026 states November 2029. The 2024 and 2026 delivery scope tables also differ, the 2026 scope being materially longer. The 2023 s16Y Ministerial Order's reasons independently state the WRL Uprate would enable additional generation capacity in Western Victoria 'by as early as 2027', bounding the likely origin of the 2024 mid-2027 figure; the Order does not state a WRL completion date and does not mention 2029. Both versions are preserved. The chart's 2029 date reflects the current representation only; the earlier schedule is not silently overwritten.
