# VNI West (Victoria)

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** AEMO's $3,499m (2020-21 dollars, inclusive of $315m WRL), its $3,614m modelling conversion and the 2026 reported $7,600m ($2025, Class 4, 'South West NSW REZ + Interconnector') are discrete whole-interconnector observations with different scope, price basis, estimate class and WRL treatment. The now-archived PACR and PADR do split their own, earlier (2023 and 2022) preferred-option estimates by state ($1,755m Victoria / $1,744m New South Wales for Option 5A; $1,605m Victoria / $1,651m New South Wales for the PADR's superseded option), but no archived source states a Victoria-only amount for the current 2026 ISP reported total, so this pack does not construct a Victoria-only headline from these different-vintage figures.

**Capacity.** 2026 ISP VIC-NSW: forward 1,890 MW, reverse 1,670 MW, REZ V1 1,580 MW and WV2 200 MW. 2024 ISP option 1: North 1,935 MW, South 1,669 MW, V2 1,580 MW, V3 200 MW, N5 900 MW.

**Completion date.** 2031-11 (VIC-NSW timing advised by proponent, 2026 ISP)

**Current status.** Actionable ISP project in the 2026 ISP. PADR published July 2022; RIT-T concluded May 2023 with the PACR confirming option 5A ($3,499m, 206 km Victorian portion); original s16Y Order gazetted February 2023 (S60), replaced by the Option 5A order in May 2023 (S267), amended by the CEO VicGrid order in November 2024 (S605) and by Special Orders substituting VicGrid in October 2025 (S590); feedback loop confirmed December 2023; EES final scoping requirements issued; early works under way; Iberdrola awarded design and development November 2025.

**Delivery status.** Early works and community engagement under way; no construction outturn, approved revenue determination or contract price is archived.

**Dependent projects.** Prerequisites stated by AEMO: VNI West - South West NSW REZ Stage 1, Victorian Western Renewables Link, HumeLink, Project EnergyConnect. WRL connects at the terminal station near Bulgana but is a separate project.

**Funding pathway.** Victorian part is a National Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005 specified augmentation (May 2023 order); the South West NSW REZ component is an actionable NSW project progressing under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW) rather than the ISP framework.

**Identity.** One integrated VNI West interconnector Project (near Bulgana - Tragowel/Kerang - Dinawan). The Victorian specified-augmentation proceeding is a component/proceeding boundary, not a Victoria-only project or cost series.

**Name.** VNI West (Victoria)

**Owner.** Joint RIT-T proponents Transgrid and AEMO Victorian Planning (AVP, now VicGrid); Transmission Company Victoria (TCV) delivers the Victorian part; Iberdrola awarded design and development in November 2025.

**Schedule.** Timing advised by proponent for the VIC-NSW component: November 2031 (2026 ISP). December 2029 capacity release date was identified in the PACR as reported in the 2024 ISP.

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2022-ISP", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S267", "VNV-VNIW-2026-OVERVIEW", "VNV-TRANSGRID-2022-PADR", "VNV-TRANSGRID-2023-PACR", "VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S060", "VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2024-S605", "VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2025-S590"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 500
