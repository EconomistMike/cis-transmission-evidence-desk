# VNI West (Victoria)

## Overview

VNV-PROJECT-VNI-VIC | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 10 sources | 44 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One integrated VNI West interconnector Project (near Bulgana - Tragowel/Kerang - Dinawan). The Victorian specified-augmentation proceeding is a component/proceeding boundary, not a Victoria-only project or cost series.

**Current status.** Actionable ISP project in the 2026 ISP. PADR published July 2022; RIT-T concluded May 2023 with the PACR confirming option 5A ($3,499m, 206 km Victorian portion); original s16Y Order gazetted February 2023 (S60), replaced by the Option 5A order in May 2023 (S267), amended by the CEO VicGrid order in November 2024 (S605) and by Special Orders substituting VicGrid in October 2025 (S590); feedback loop confirmed December 2023; EES final scoping requirements issued; early works under way; Iberdrola awarded design and development November 2025.

**Capacity.** 2026 ISP VIC-NSW: forward 1,890 MW, reverse 1,670 MW, REZ V1 1,580 MW and WV2 200 MW. 2024 ISP option 1: North 1,935 MW, South 1,669 MW, V2 1,580 MW, V3 200 MW, N5 900 MW.

**Schedule.** Timing advised by proponent for the VIC-NSW component: November 2031 (2026 ISP). December 2029 capacity release date was identified in the PACR as reported in the 2024 ISP.

**Owner.** Joint RIT-T proponents Transgrid and AEMO Victorian Planning (AVP, now VicGrid); Transmission Company Victoria (TCV) delivers the Victorian part; Iberdrola awarded design and development in November 2025.

**Funding pathway.** Victorian part is a National Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005 specified augmentation (May 2023 order); the South West NSW REZ component is an actionable NSW project progressing under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW) rather than the ISP framework.

**Delivery status.** Early works and community engagement under way; no construction outturn, approved revenue determination or contract price is archived.

**Dependent projects.** Prerequisites stated by AEMO: VNI West - South West NSW REZ Stage 1, Victorian Western Renewables Link, HumeLink, Project EnergyConnect. WRL connects at the terminal station near Bulgana but is a separate project.

## Timeline

- 2022-06-30 — 2022 ISP candidate option (planner): VNI West (via Kerang) identified as the ISP candidate option in the ODP; described as a 500 kV interconnector from a substation near Ballarat in Victoria to a new substation named Dinawan in southwest New South Wales, with Stage 2 implementation by July 2031. [HUM-AEMO-2022-ISP; 74]
- 2023-05 — RIT-T concluded; option 5A preferred; NEVA specified augmentation order (decision, primary instruments now archived): AVP and Transgrid concluded the joint RIT-T and confirmed Option 5A as the preferred option: estimated capital cost $3,499m (June 2021 real dollars / FY2020-21 dollars, Class 4, ±30%), split $1,755m Victoria / $1,744m New South Wales; indicative transfer capability +1,935 MW Victoria to NSW and +1,669 MW NSW to Victoria; approximate line length 206 km in Victoria and 203 km in New South Wales (route not yet determined). In the same month the Victorian Minister for Energy and Resources issued the s16Y Order (27 May 2023, Special Gazette S267) identifying Option 5A as the specified augmentation, replacing the original 20 February 2023 order. The PACR and both gazetted orders are now archived. [VNV-TRANSGRID-2023-PACR; 9, 48, 88]
- 2023-12 — AEMO feedback loop confirmation (reported, primary instrument unavailable): AEMO provided feedback loop confirmation to Transgrid that the project addresses the identified need and aligns with the optimal development path in the most recent ISP. The notice itself was not captured. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 52]
- 2024-06 — 2024 ISP actionable project representation (planner): Option 1 scope published with $3,499m in 2020-21 dollars inclusive of $315m WRL, a $3,614m modelling conversion, Class 4 (± 30%), capacity North 1,935 MW / South 1,669 MW / V2 1,580 MW / V3 200 MW / N5 900 MW, and a December 2029 PACR capacity release date. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; 44-45]
- 2025 — EES final scoping requirements issued (reported, primary instrument unavailable): The Minister issued the final scoping requirements for the Environment Effects Statement for VNI West after considering public comments on the draft. The EES record itself is unavailable. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 52]
- 2023-05-27 — VNI West and WRL Ministerial Order gazetted under s16Y NEVA (primary instrument now archived): Minister Lily D'Ambrosio made the Order under s16Y of the National Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005 on 26 May 2023, published in Special Gazette S267 on 27 May 2023 (having consulted the Premier, Treasurer and AEMO under s16ZA). Schedule 1 specifies Option 5A as the specified augmentation for VNI West: a new 500 kV double-circuit overhead line from a new terminal station near Kerang to Dinawan and to Bulgana Terminal Station, with two 500/220 kV 1,000 MVA transformers and up to +/- 400 MVAr dynamic reactive compensation at the Kerang terminal station, plus two new 500 kV bays at Bulgana Terminal Station. Schedule 2 specifies the WRL Uprate as the specified augmentation for WRL, including relocating the terminal station previously proposed for Mount Prospect (north of Ballarat) to Bulgana and uprating that segment from 220 kV to 500 kV. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S267; 1, 3, 6-8]
- 2024-06 — EPBC Act referral confirmed as a Controlled Action (government): The Commonwealth Minister for the Environment and Water confirmed VNI West is a 'Controlled Action' requiring assessment and approval under the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999, to be assessed under the bilateral agreement relying on Victoria's EES process. [VNV-VNIW-2026-OVERVIEW; web]
- 2025-02-17 — Victorian electricity transmission licence granted (regulatory approval): The Essential Services Commission granted the VicGrid VNI West project a Victorian electricity transmission licence, following a public consultation process that ran from 5 September 2024 to 11 November 2024. [VNV-VNIW-2026-OVERVIEW; web]
- 2025-11-01 — Victorian network functions transfer to VicGrid (proponent change): AEMO's Victorian network functions transferred to VicGrid Body Corporate, including responsibility for Transmission Company Victoria and the VNI West project. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51]
- 2025-11 — Iberdrola design and development award (proponent): Iberdrola awarded the contract to design and develop the project in collaboration with TCV and subsequently submit a proposal to build, own and operate the transmission line. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 52]
- 2026-06 — NSW Government South West NSW REZ investment announcement (government): NSW announced investment to support critical grid upgrades for the South West New South Wales REZ; the component is accordingly categorised as an actionable NSW project under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW). [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51]
- 2026-06 — 2026 ISP representation (current planner evidence): VNI West remains actionable; AEMO reconfirms single integrated delivery rather than staging the South West NSW REZ component; reported cost $7,600m ($2025) Class 4 (-30%/+50%) for 'South West NSW REZ + Interconnector', of which $565m is approved early works and other incurred costs; VIC-NSW timing advised as November 2031. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51-53]
- 2022-07 — VNI West RIT-T Project Assessment Draft Report (PADR) published (planner, primary instrument now archived): AVP and Transgrid published the joint VNI West PADR (dated 29 July 2022 per the later s16Y Order's definitions). It assessed two credible options and preferred Option 1 (VNI West, via north of Ballarat - near Bendigo - near Kerang - Dinawan) over Option 2 (a Victorian Transmission Link brought forward ahead of VNI West): approximately 400 km of double-circuit 500 kV line at an estimated capital cost of $3,256m (FY2020-21 dollars, Class 4, ±30%; $1,605m Victoria / $1,651m New South Wales), with indicative transfer capacity +1,930 MW Victoria to NSW and +1,800 MW NSW to Victoria. This preferred route was later superseded by Option 5A in the May 2023 PACR. [VNV-TRANSGRID-2022-PADR; 1, 53, 95]
- 2023-02-20 — Original VNI West and WRL Ministerial Order gazetted under s16Y NEVA (primary instrument now archived): Minister Lily D'Ambrosio made the first s16Y Order dated 15 February 2023, published in Special Gazette S60 on 20 February 2023, ahead of the RIT-T's conclusion. It specified VNI West (a new high-capacity transmission line connecting WRL with Project EnergyConnect, per the PADR/PACR identified need) and WRL as specified augmentations, and conferred functions on AEMO including assessing alternate options (alternate routes, nodes, terminal stations and network design) and commencing early works, ahead of a further order or the VNI West construction agreement. This order was replaced by the 27 May 2023 Order once Option 5A was confirmed as preferred. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2023-S060; 1, 3]
- 2024-11-08 — CEO VicGrid to assist AEMO (VNI West) Ministerial Order gazetted (primary instrument now archived): Minister Lily D'Ambrosio made a further s16Y Order dated 4 November 2024, published in Special Gazette S605 on 8 November 2024 (a matching order was also made for WRL), amending the 20 February 2023 and 27 May 2023 Orders to confer functions on the CEO VicGrid to assist AEMO in carrying out VNI West functions, ahead of VicGrid's later assumption of AEMO's Victorian network functions. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2024-S605; 1, 5]
- 2025-10-28 — Special Orders substitute VicGrid for AEMO/CEO VicGrid across the VNI West and WRL Ministerial Orders (primary instrument now archived): Minister Lily D'Ambrosio made four Special Orders under s16ZHA of the Act, each dated 15 October 2025 and published in Special Gazette S590 on 28 October 2025, commencing 1 November 2025: amending the 20 February 2023 Order (S60), the 27 May 2023 Order (S267), and both CEO VicGrid Orders of 8 November 2024 (S605, VNI West and WRL) to substitute 'VicGrid' (now the VicGrid Body Corporate established under the State Owned Enterprises Act 1992) for 'AEMO'/'CEO VicGrid' throughout, reflecting VicGrid's assumption of AEMO's Victorian network functions. This is the primary legal instrument for the 1 November 2025 transfer already reported by the 2026 ISP. [VNV-VIC-GAZETTE-2025-S590; 1-5]

## Warnings and open decisions

- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#2 (blocker): No archived source publishes a Victoria/NSW allocation of the current (2026 ISP) reported VNI West cost. AEMO's 2026 ISP reports a single integrated project cost spanning 'South West NSW REZ + Interconnector' and explicitly finds integrated delivery more efficient than staging the NSW component. The now-archived PACR and PADR do split their own, earlier (2023 and 2022) preferred-option estimates by state ($1,755m Victoria / $1,744m New South Wales for Option 5A, FY2020-21 dollars; $1,605m Victoria / $1,651m New South Wales for the PADR's superseded Option 1), but these are a different vintage, price basis and RIT-T scope from the 2026 ISP's $7,600m reported total. The author must still decide whether to publish a Victoria-only headline cost at all. This pack does not construct one: an even halving of the $7,600m remains an author construction, and substituting the PACR's/PADR's older, RIT-T-vintage Victoria sub-totals for the current chart cell would mix price bases and scopes without a stated basis for doing so.
