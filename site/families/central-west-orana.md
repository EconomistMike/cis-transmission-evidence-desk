# Central-West Orana Renewable Energy Zone

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** The family cannot be represented by one cost series or one row. The published Main, Enabling and Expansion amounts have different price bases, payer boundaries, maturities and dates, and the chart's $6.787 billion is an arithmetic aggregate that no source publishes and that still omits generator-funded H2P and system strength, construction finance and the $186.8m Barigan Creek acquisition.

**Capacity.** Declared intended 6,000 MW (amended from 3,000 MW in December 2023), initial delivery and determined transfer capacity 4,500 MW, hosting up to 7.7 GW, aggregate access cap 7.7 GW, awarded access rights reported as both 7.15 GW and 7.7 GW, Expansion +1,500 MW.

**Completion date.** 2028-12 (committed project per AEMO); staged April 2028 to April 2030 per the AER; Expansion March 2033

**Current status.** Main committed and under construction since June 2025 after financial close on 4 April 2025, with a remade AER revenue determination; Enabling finalised by the AER in January 2026; Expansion actionable under the NSW EII Act with no authorisation or determination observed.

**Delivery status.** Main: committed, under construction, post financial close, subject to 29 adjustment mechanisms and five-yearly remakes. Enabling: determined for 2026-31, excluding Barigan Creek incremental expenditure. Expansion: actionable, no proponent authorisation or determination observed.

**Dependent projects.** Hunter Transmission Project (CNSW-SNW Option 1) is a prerequisite once the REZ exceeds 3 GW; the Expansion is a prerequisite-dependent follow-on to the committed Main project; the Enabling project enables the Main project's connection to the shared network.

**Funding pathway.** NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020: EnergyCo recommendation, Consumer Trustee authorisation and AER revenue determination for consumer-funded amounts; access right holders fund hub-to-project and centralised system strength through per-connection access fees; a CEFC loan supports land and biodiversity costs that are ultimately recovered from consumers.

**Identity.** One project family with three separately authorised or planned projects: the Main CWO REZ network project (ACEREZ), the Enabling CWO REZ network project (Transgrid) and the actionable CWO REZ Expansion. Barigan Creek Switching Station moves between the first two; Bannaby is a statutory inclusion with no observed cost attribution.

**Name.** Central-West Orana Renewable Energy Zone

**Owner.** ACEREZ Partnership (Acciona, Cobra and Endeavour Energy) for the Main project for 35 years from financial close; Transgrid for the Enabling project and, after transfer, for the Barigan Creek Switching Station; EnergyCo NSW as infrastructure planner.

**Schedule.** Committed project December 2028 per AEMO; staged infrastructure completion April 2028 to April 2030 per the AER; Expansion timing advised by proponent March 2033.

**Source ids.** ["CWO-01", "CWO-02", "CWO-04", "CWO-05", "CWO-06", "CWO-07", "CWO-08", "CWO-09", "CWO-10", "CWO-11", "CWO-12", "CWO-13", "CWO-14", "CWO-15", "CWO-16", "CWO-17", "CWO-18", "CWO-19", "CWO-20", "CWO-21", "CWO-22", "CWO-23", "CWO-24", "CWO-25", "CWO-26", "CWO-27"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 500
