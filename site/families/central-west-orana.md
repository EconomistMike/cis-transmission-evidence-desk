# Central-West Orana Renewable Energy Zone

## Overview

CWO-PROJECT-MAIN | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 26 sources | 64 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One project family with three separately authorised or planned projects: the Main CWO REZ network project (ACEREZ), the Enabling CWO REZ network project (Transgrid) and the actionable CWO REZ Expansion. Barigan Creek Switching Station moves between the first two; Bannaby is a statutory inclusion with no observed cost attribution.

**Current status.** Main committed and under construction since June 2025 after financial close on 4 April 2025, with a remade AER revenue determination; Enabling finalised by the AER in January 2026; Expansion actionable under the NSW EII Act with no authorisation or determination observed.

**Capacity.** Declared intended 6,000 MW (amended from 3,000 MW in December 2023), initial delivery and determined transfer capacity 4,500 MW, hosting up to 7.7 GW, aggregate access cap 7.7 GW, awarded access rights reported as both 7.15 GW and 7.7 GW, Expansion +1,500 MW.

**Schedule.** Committed project December 2028 per AEMO; staged infrastructure completion April 2028 to April 2030 per the AER; Expansion timing advised by proponent March 2033.

**Owner.** ACEREZ Partnership (Acciona, Cobra and Endeavour Energy) for the Main project for 35 years from financial close; Transgrid for the Enabling project and, after transfer, for the Barigan Creek Switching Station; EnergyCo NSW as infrastructure planner.

**Funding pathway.** NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020: EnergyCo recommendation, Consumer Trustee authorisation and AER revenue determination for consumer-funded amounts; access right holders fund hub-to-project and centralised system strength through per-connection access fees; a CEFC loan supports land and biodiversity costs that are ultimately recovered from consumers.

**Delivery status.** Main: committed, under construction, post financial close, subject to 29 adjustment mechanisms and five-yearly remakes. Enabling: determined for 2026-31, excluding Barigan Creek incremental expenditure. Expansion: actionable, no proponent authorisation or determination observed.

**Dependent projects.** Hunter Transmission Project (CNSW-SNW Option 1) is a prerequisite once the REZ exceeds 3 GW; the Expansion is a prerequisite-dependent follow-on to the committed Main project; the Enabling project enables the Main project's connection to the shared network.

## Warnings and open decisions

- NON_ADDITIVE_BOUNDARIES#1 (blocker): The chart's $6.787 billion is the sum of $5,526.1m (Main, nominal NER-equivalent, consumer-funded, excluding H2P/CSS and construction finance), $406.0m (Enabling, real 2025-26 capex) and $855m (Expansion, dollar year not stated, Class 5b, not yet authorised or determined). The three amounts have different price bases, payer boundaries, maturities and delivery dates, and the total still omits generator-funded H2P and CSS, construction finance and the $186.8m Barigan Creek acquisition. Publish the three observations side by side rather than one total, or label the total as an arithmetic aggregate that is not a scope-complete cost. [CWO-18; CWO-21; CWO-22; CWO-18 PDF p38; CWO-21 PDF pp7, 50; CWO-22 PDF p57]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#14 (warning): The chart's date cell reads 2029. No archived source states 2029: AEMO gives December 2028 for the committed project, the AER gives staged infrastructure completion between April 2028 and April 2030 with generation from December 2028 to September 2030, and the Expansion whose cost is in the same row is timed March 2033. The author must choose which milestone the date column represents and whether one date can stand for a row that aggregates three projects. [CWO-18; CWO-22; CWO-18 PDF p12; CWO-22 PDF pp23, 57]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#15 (warning): The chart's hubs cell reads 3, counting Merotherie-A, Merotherie-B and Elong Elong across the committed project and the future Expansion. Archived sources name two energy hubs (Merotherie and Elong Elong) for the committed project and treat Barigan Creek as a switching station. The author must confirm the counting rule and whether future Expansion assets should be counted in the same cell as committed assets. [CWO-18; CWO-22; CWO-18 PDF pp11, 14; CWO-22 PDF pp23, 57]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#16 (warning): The alt routes cell reads 0. No archived source assesses route redundancy or alternative routes for this project; the value is an author definition. The author must record the definition of an alternative route and confirm that two double circuit lines within one corridor are being counted as no alternative route. [No archived source addresses alt routes]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#17 (warning): The Bannaby 500 kV substation is inside the statutory REZ network infrastructure description from November 2025 but has no observed funding pathway, operator or cost attribution, and no cost is allocated to it in this pack. - NON_COMPARABLE_SERIES#2 (warning): The 2020 ($650m modelled), 2023 ($3.2b real 2021), 2024 (~$5.45b nominal Feb 2024), 2025 ($5,526.1m nominal Main only) and 2026 ($855m Expansion) amounts differ in scope, corridor, price basis, payer and maturity. EnergyCo states expressly that the 2020 estimate is out of date because of an added double circuit, a corridor move and an enlarged project. [CWO-01; CWO-04; CWO-09; CWO-18; CWO-22; CWO-01 PDF pp14, 31; CWO-04 PDF pp31-32; CWO-09 PDF pp11-12; CWO-18 PDF p38; CWO-22 PDF p57]
