# Hunter Transmission Project

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** The $1,364m (2026 ISP), $1,099m (2024 ISP) and about $1 billion (NSW Network Infrastructure Strategy, May 2023 Stage 1) amounts are separate dated observations on different bases, price years and scopes. The $265m difference between the two ISP amounts is not an overrun and no arithmetic comparison is promoted.

**Capacity.** AEMO 2026 reports forward additional network capacity of 5,000 MW (REZ N10: 2,000 MW); AEMO 2024 reported the same up-to-5,000 MW observation. This is transfer-capacity/hosting wording, not a rating of the single line.

**Completion date.** 2029-11

**Current status.** Actionable New South Wales project under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW); AEMO reports the project is in the NSW Government's independent assessment phase, with construction expected to start in late 2026 if approved. No EnergyCo approval, business case or statutory authorisation record was captured.

**Delivery status.** Pre-construction; NSW Government independent assessment phase per AEMO 2026.

**Dependent projects.** AEMO makes CNSW-SNW Option 1 (HTP) a pre-requisite for the Central-West Orana REZ network project above 3 GW and for the Sydney Ring South power flow control augmentation (CNSW-SNW Option 2d).

**Funding pathway.** Priority transmission infrastructure project under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW); progresses as an actionable New South Wales project rather than under the RIT-T/ISP framework.

**Identity.** One provisional Project, also named Sydney Ring North (AEMO CNSW-SNW Option 1). AEMO's 2026 representation bundles the Waratah Super Battery and related upgrades; WSB is treated as provisionally distinct because its own lifecycle records were not captured.

**Name.** Hunter Transmission Project

**Owner.** EnergyCo NSW is the Infrastructure Planner for the project as a priority transmission infrastructure project; no EnergyCo delivery record was captured.

**Schedule.** Timing advised by proponent: November 2029 (2026 ISP); December 2028 (2024 ISP).

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2022-ISP", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "CWO-04", "HTP-ENERGYCO-2026-INFRA-PAGE", "HTP-ENERGYCO-2026-PROJECT-PAGE", "HTP-AER-2025-FINALDECISION", "HTP-NSWGOV-2022-GAZETTE-569"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 500
