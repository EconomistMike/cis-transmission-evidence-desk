# HumeLink

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** HumeLink cannot be represented by one cost series or one topology row. Published amounts move across estimate maturity (AACE Class 4 to Class 2-3), price bases ($2017-18, $2020-21, $2022-23, June 2021 and June 2023 dollars), scope boundaries (option, stage, whole project, delivery package, planning development) and publisher roles (proponent estimate, planner expected cost, feedback-loop confirmation, regulatory allowance, planning capital investment value). The only defensible same-basis movement published in the archive is Transgrid's 28 per cent increase from $3,820m to $4,881m in June 2023 dollars; the AER's $4,573.5m all-stage allowance is a regulatory substitution, not forecast error; and the December 2027 date is still a forecast, so no retrospective accuracy measure is yet possible.

**Capacity.** AEMO 2026: transfer capability between SNSW and CNSW increases by 2,200 MW in both directions once in service (2,200 MW in both directions in the 2024 ISP), plus REZ figures of 400 MW in N5 and 2,200 MW in N6+N7. RIT-T-era observations state 2,570 MW of additional firm capacity or generation accommodated (PADR and PACR) and 2,230-2,570 MW of Tumut to Sydney transfer uplift (2020 ISP). These are different measures, not one series.

**Completion date.** 2027-12 (expected)

**Current status.** Committed. The AER made a Stage 2 contingent project determination on 2 August 2024 and NSW granted critical State significant infrastructure approval on 13 November 2024; AEMO's 2026 ISP lists the project as committed and under delivery. No outturn or commissioning record exists.

**Delivery status.** Committed and under delivery; construction expected to take about 2.5 years per the NSW approval. No final as-built scope, commissioning result or audited outturn is published.

**Dependent projects.** Switching Station Near Wondalga (separate actionable ISP project; HumeLink is its stated prerequisite). Snowy 2.0 and Project EnergyConnect are the generation and interconnection connections HumeLink serves. Sydney Ring works are related system projects, not deleted HumeLink components; the PADR's power-flow-control options between Bannaby and Sydney were not required in Option 3C's then-current design.

**Funding pathway.** Actionable ISP project: RIT-T (PSCR 2019, amended PADR 2020, PACR and addendum 2021), AEMO feedback loops (2022, 2023 x2, 2024), and staged NER contingent project applications determined by the AER (Stage 1 Part 1 August 2022, Stage 1 Part 2 August 2023, Stage 2 August 2024). Physical approval is separate, under the NSW critical State significant infrastructure regime.

**Identity.** One project family. HumeLink descends from the 2018 SnowyLink North concept (a Wagga-Bannaby-Snowy 2.0-Wagga single-circuit 500 kV loop inside a broader SnowyLink program), but no archived source states that all of SnowyLink became HumeLink. The Switching Station Near Wondalga is a formally separate actionable ISP project that names HumeLink as its prerequisite and is held here as a related component, not as HumeLink scope.

**Name.** HumeLink

**Owner.** Transgrid

**Schedule.** Expected completion December 2027 (2026 ISP). Earlier dated targets: 2025-26 (2020 ISP), July 2026 (2022 ISP), Northern Circuit July 2026 and Southern Circuit December 2026 (feedback loop 3 and 2024 ISP).
