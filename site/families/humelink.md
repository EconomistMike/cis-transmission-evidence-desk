# HumeLink

## Overview

HUM-PROJECT-HUMELINK | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 27 sources | 91 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One project family. HumeLink descends from the 2018 SnowyLink North concept (a Wagga-Bannaby-Snowy 2.0-Wagga single-circuit 500 kV loop inside a broader SnowyLink program), but no archived source states that all of SnowyLink became HumeLink. The Switching Station Near Wondalga is a formally separate actionable ISP project that names HumeLink as its prerequisite and is held here as a related component, not as HumeLink scope.

**Current status.** Committed. The AER made a Stage 2 contingent project determination on 2 August 2024 and NSW granted critical State significant infrastructure approval on 13 November 2024; AEMO's 2026 ISP lists the project as committed and under delivery. No outturn or commissioning record exists.

**Capacity.** AEMO 2026: transfer capability between SNSW and CNSW increases by 2,200 MW in both directions once in service (2,200 MW in both directions in the 2024 ISP), plus REZ figures of 400 MW in N5 and 2,200 MW in N6+N7. RIT-T-era observations state 2,570 MW of additional firm capacity or generation accommodated (PADR and PACR) and 2,230-2,570 MW of Tumut to Sydney transfer uplift (2020 ISP). These are different measures, not one series.

**Schedule.** Expected completion December 2027 (2026 ISP). Earlier dated targets: 2025-26 (2020 ISP), July 2026 (2022 ISP), Northern Circuit July 2026 and Southern Circuit December 2026 (feedback loop 3 and 2024 ISP).

**Owner.** Transgrid

**Funding pathway.** Actionable ISP project: RIT-T (PSCR 2019, amended PADR 2020, PACR and addendum 2021), AEMO feedback loops (2022, 2023 x2, 2024), and staged NER contingent project applications determined by the AER (Stage 1 Part 1 August 2022, Stage 1 Part 2 August 2023, Stage 2 August 2024). Physical approval is separate, under the NSW critical State significant infrastructure regime.

**Delivery status.** Committed and under delivery; construction expected to take about 2.5 years per the NSW approval. No final as-built scope, commissioning result or audited outturn is published.

**Dependent projects.** Switching Station Near Wondalga (separate actionable ISP project; HumeLink is its stated prerequisite). Snowy 2.0 and Project EnergyConnect are the generation and interconnection connections HumeLink serves. Sydney Ring works are related system projects, not deleted HumeLink components; the PADR's power-flow-control options between Bannaby and Sydney were not required in Option 3C's then-current design.

## Timeline

- 2018-07 — SnowyLink North identified as a predecessor concept (ISP development): AEMO described a new link from Tumut to Bannaby ('SnowyLink North') and associated Bannaby to Sydney West works as providing benefits from the mid-2020s, deliverable as the northern stage of a two-stage SnowyLink. [HUM-AEMO-2018-ISP; 9, 80]
- 2018-07 — Single-circuit 500 kV loop described in the ISP appendices (ISP development): Appendix D listed installation of a Wagga-Bannaby-Snowy 2.0-Wagga single circuit 500 kV loop within the broader SnowyLink program. The documents do not state that all of SnowyLink later became HumeLink. [HUM-AEMO-2018-ISP-APP; 61]
- 2019-06-25 — PSCR published, commencing the HumeLink RIT-T (proponent / RIT-T stage 1): Transgrid issued the Project Specification Consultation Report for reinforcing the New South Wales southern shared network to increase transfer capacity to the state's demand centres. [HUM-TG-2019-PSCR; 1]
- 2020-01-15 — Amended PADR prefers Option 3C (proponent / RIT-T stage 2): Option 3C, new 500 kV route diverse lines in a loop between Maragle, Bannaby and Wagga Wagga, at approximately $1,350 million with 2,570 MW of additional generation accommodated. Route diversity was treated as an opportunity, with final diversity decisions deferred. [HUM-TG-2020-PADR; 6, 27]
- 2020-07-30 — HumeLink made actionable in the 2020 ISP (planner): Approximately 630 km of upgrades, a $1,470m to $2,730m cost range with a $2,100m modelled value, 2,230-2,570 MW Tumut to Sydney transfer uplift, timed for 2025-26 across all scenarios. [HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3; 14, 29-30]
- 2021-07-29 — PACR retains Option 3C and changes all lines to double-circuit (proponent / RIT-T conclusion): All lines are to be constructed in a double-circuit configuration, described as a change since the PADR and a refinement of the ISP candidate option. Option 3C is $3,317m ($2020/21) with $491m weighted net benefits; variants 3C-0 ($4,253m) and 3C-1 ($3,509m) are electrically identical but physically and financially different. [HUM-TG-2021-PACR; 2, 34, 61]
- 2021-08-16 — Wunelli lodges a RIT-T dispute notice (participant allegation, not a finding): Wunelli alleged the PACR failed to identify at least two distinct configurations of the preferred loop option with materially different route lengths, risks, construction costs and network benefits, and that the PACR topology figure was misleading. [HUM-WUN-2021-DISPUTE; 1-2]
- 2021-09-01 — Transgrid answers the AER's information request (proponent clarification): All-double-circuit Option 3C means two circuits on one line of towers; all-single-circuit Option 3C-0 means two parallel lines of towers. Transgrid confirmed a complete double circuit configuration between all three substation locations, and stated biodiversity costs for 3C of $953m. [HUM-TG-2021-RFI1; 2]
- 2021-09 — Route development changes announced (proponent project update): The proposed Book Book to Gobarralong and Maragle to Yaven Creek lines were no longer required; a new connection to the Blowering corridor was introduced through Gilmore Valley, and a new corridor option north of Tumut was to be explored. [HUM-TG-2021-NEWS-SEP; 2]
- 2021-11-24 — AER dispute determination requires Option 1C-new (regulator finding): The AER determined that Transgrid did not meet the RIT-T requirements on credible options and required the PACR to be amended to include a full double circuit Maragle-Bannaby option (Option 1C-new) with costs, comparative cost-benefit analysis and sensitivities. It was satisfied other configurations of the top two options need not be assessed, and noted that materially higher route-finalised costs could constitute a material change in circumstances. [HUM-AER-2021-DISPUTE-DETERMINATION; 5, 20, 24-26]
- 2021-12-17 — PACR addendum assesses Option 1C-new; Option 3C remains preferred (proponent / RIT-T concluded): Option 1C-new: a double circuit 500 kV line between Maragle and Bannaby (274 km) at approximately $2,695m including $858m biodiversity, an AACE Class 4 P50 estimate with typical accuracy of L -15% to -30% and H +20% to +50%.
