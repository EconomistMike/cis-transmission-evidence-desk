# VNI West (South West NSW REZ component)

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** The identical $3.7bn NSW and Victoria chart entries have no evidenced current state allocation. $3.7bn is not half of the 2026 $7.6bn whole-VNI total (half is $3.8bn). The author's exact derivation is unresolved. Historical PACR Option 5A allocations ($1,755m Victoria / $1,744m NSW, FY2020-21, p48) do not allocate the 2026 total; NSW $948.8m approved early works is not a NSW whole-project estimate.

**Capacity.** AEMO 2026 reports REZ N5: 600 MW, plus a separately modelled +1,800 MW REZ transfer from Dinawan conditional on a third Dinawan transformer (N5 Option 2). The interconnector Stage 2 adds forward 1,890 MW / reverse 1,670 MW.

**Completion date.** South West NSW REZ component: August 2029 (proponent advice, 2026 ISP). Whole VIC-NSW link: November 2031.

**Current status.** Actionable NSW project progressing under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW); RIT-T concluded May 2023 (option 5A) and feedback loop confirmed December 2023; early works under way.

**Delivery status.** Early works, community engagement and technical studies; Iberdrola awarded the design-and-develop contract in November 2025 (Victorian side, with TCV). No NSW construction outturn captured.

**Dependent projects.** HumeLink and Project EnergyConnect are stated prerequisites for the South West NSW REZ Stage 1 scope; Western Renewables Link is a prerequisite for Stage 2 and its cost was bundled into the 2024 $3,499m figure.

**Funding pathway.** NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 pathway for the South West NSW REZ component; NSW Government announced $225m in the 2026 NSW Budget (funding announcement, not project cost, allowance or outturn).

**Identity.** AEMO treats VNI West as one integrated 500 kV interconnector from near Bulgana (Vic) to Dinawan (NSW); the South West NSW REZ component is separately categorised as an actionable NSW project. This pack curates the NSW component; no NSW-only cost, route or capacity is excised from the whole-project figures.

**Name.** VNI West (South West NSW REZ component)

**Owner.** Transgrid (NSW) with VicGrid/TCV in Victoria; Transgrid and AVP (now VicGrid) were the RIT-T proponents. Transgrid's own scope/approval records were unavailable.

**Schedule.** Proponent timing for the South West NSW REZ component is August 2029 (earlier AEMO assumption June 2030); VIC-NSW Stage 2 proponent timing is November 2031.

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "ATE15-EC-FUND-1", "VNN-AER-2024-CPADETERMINATION", "VNN-TG-2026-PROJECTPAGE", "VNN-ENERGYCO-2026-SWREZ-TRANSMISSION", "VNV-TRANSGRID-2023-PACR", "VNV-TRANSGRID-2022-PADR"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 500 kV (interconnector); NSW component upgrades the Dinawan - Wagga Wagga - Gugaa double-circuit line from 330 kV to 500 kV operation.
