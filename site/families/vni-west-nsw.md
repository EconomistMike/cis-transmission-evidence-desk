# VNI West (South West NSW REZ component)

## Overview

VNN-PROJECT-VNI-NSW | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 9 sources | 28 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** AEMO treats VNI West as one integrated 500 kV interconnector from near Bulgana (Vic) to Dinawan (NSW); the South West NSW REZ component is separately categorised as an actionable NSW project. This pack curates the NSW component; no NSW-only cost, route or capacity is excised from the whole-project figures.

**Current status.** Actionable NSW project progressing under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW); RIT-T concluded May 2023 (option 5A) and feedback loop confirmed December 2023; early works under way.

**Capacity.** AEMO 2026 reports REZ N5: 600 MW, plus a separately modelled +1,800 MW REZ transfer from Dinawan conditional on a third Dinawan transformer (N5 Option 2). The interconnector Stage 2 adds forward 1,890 MW / reverse 1,670 MW.

**Schedule.** Proponent timing for the South West NSW REZ component is August 2029 (earlier AEMO assumption June 2030); VIC-NSW Stage 2 proponent timing is November 2031.

**Owner.** Transgrid (NSW) with VicGrid/TCV in Victoria; Transgrid and AVP (now VicGrid) were the RIT-T proponents. Transgrid's own scope/approval records were unavailable.

**Funding pathway.** NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 pathway for the South West NSW REZ component; NSW Government announced $225m in the 2026 NSW Budget (funding announcement, not project cost, allowance or outturn).

**Delivery status.** Early works, community engagement and technical studies; Iberdrola awarded the design-and-develop contract in November 2025 (Victorian side, with TCV). No NSW construction outturn captured.

**Dependent projects.** HumeLink and Project EnergyConnect are stated prerequisites for the South West NSW REZ Stage 1 scope; Western Renewables Link is a prerequisite for Stage 2 and its cost was bundled into the 2024 $3,499m figure.

## Timeline

- 2020-07 — 2020 ISP presents VNI West route alternatives (historical candidate options): Shepparton and Kerang routes assessed as ISP candidate options; the Kerang route reports South West NSW REZ hosting +1,000 MW and VIC-NSW transfer +1,930 MW north / +1,800 MW south. [HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3; 33-36]
- 2023-05 — RIT-T concluded; option 5A confirmed as preferred option (proponent decision, reported by AEMO): AVP and Transgrid concluded the RIT-T and confirmed option 5A (VNI West PACR). The Victorian Minister also issued an order identifying VNI West as a specified augmentation for the Victorian part of the project. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51]
- 2023-05 — VNI West PACR published; Option 5A capital cost stated at $3,499m ($m 2020-21) and NSW-portion line length assumed at 184 km (proponent report, primary PACR now archived): AVP/Transgrid's RIT-T Project Assessment Conclusions Report Volume 1 confirms Option 5A (north of Kerang) as preferred, with whole-interconnector capital cost $3,499m ($m 2020-21), and assumes an NSW-portion line length of 184 km (85% on private land) for Strategic Benefits Payment Scheme opex estimation. [VNV-TRANSGRID-2023-PACR; 10, 116]
- 2023-12 — AEMO feedback loop confirmation to Transgrid (planner confirmation): AEMO confirmed the project addresses the identified need and aligns with the optimal development path in the most recent ISP. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 52]
- 2023-12 — Transgrid submits VNI West Stage 1 early works contingent project application to the AER (proponent regulatory application): Transgrid's CPA proposed $1,096.3 million ($Real 2022-23) in capex for the New South Wales portion of the project and $213.4 million in incremental revenue for the 2023-28 regulatory period. [VNN-AER-2024-CPADETERMINATION; 3-4]
- 2024-05 — AER determines VNI West Stage 1 early works contingent project (regulatory determination): AER substitutes an alternative estimate of $948.8 million ($Real 2022-23) for Transgrid's proposed $1,096.3 million stage 1 early works capex, and approves $164.2 million incremental revenue over 2025-26 to 2027-28. [VNN-AER-2024-CPADETERMINATION; 6-7]
- 2024-06 — 2024 ISP actionable representation (planner): Whole VNI West Option 1 scope with two Dinawan 500/330 kV 1,500 MVA transformers; $3,499m in 2020-21 dollars inclusive of WRL, and $3,614m applied for ISP modelling; PACR capacity release date December 2029. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; 44-45]
- 2025-08 — EIS for the NSW section of VNI West placed on public exhibition (planning process milestone, proponent-stated): Transgrid's project page states the EIS for the NSW section was placed on public exhibition in August 2025; an Amendment Report and Submissions Report were subsequently lodged with NSW DPHI. [VNN-TG-2026-PROJECTPAGE; captured HTML page; derivative line 176-177]
- 2025-11 — Iberdrola awarded design-and-develop contract (reported by AEMO; primary contract not archived): Iberdrola awarded the contract to design and develop the project with TCV and subsequently submit a proposal to build, own and operate the transmission line. AEMO's Victorian network functions also transferred to VicGrid from 1 November 2025. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51-52]
- 2026-06 — NSW Government announces $225m South West REZ investment (government announcement): The 2026 NSW Budget commits $225 million to support South West REZ grid upgrades; captured from the official EnergyCo route, with publication date not asserted. [ATE15-EC-FUND-1; captured HTML page; derivative line 272-278]
- 2026-06 — South West NSW REZ component categorised as an actionable NSW project (planner categorisation / lifecycle change): Following the NSW investment announcement, the component progresses under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW) rather than the ISP framework, while VNI West remains one actionable integrated ISP project; reported cost $7,600m ($2025, Class 4 -30%/+50%) with a separate $116m third-transformer increment. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; 51-52]
- 2026 — EnergyCo NSW names VNI West as one of two South West REZ transmission projects (delivery-body confirmation): EnergyCo's South West REZ transmission and generation projects page names Project EnergyConnect and VNI West as the two transmission projects forming the REZ's access rights network, and states Transgrid is currently planning VNI West. [VNN-ENERGYCO-2026-SWREZ-TRANSMISSION; captured HTML page; derivative line 284-315]
- 2026 — NSW DPHI reviewing EIS for VNI West; approval decision anticipated late 2026 (planning process, anticipated timing): Transgrid's project page states NSW DPHI is reviewing the EIS, Amendment Report and Submissions Report before making a recommendation towards a final approval decision by the State and Commonwealth governments, with a decision anticipated in late 2026. [VNN-TG-2026-PROJECTPAGE; captured HTML page; derivative line 185]

## Warnings and open decisions

- CHART_DERIVATION#1 (blocker): The chart's 3.7 cost is half of a whole-interconnector amount: the VNI West (NSW) and VNI West (VIC) rows carry the identical 3.7, and no archived source states a NSW-only cost. The only current whole-VNI figure, $7,600m ($2025, Class 4, two transformers), halves to 3.8, so the halved input cannot be identified in the archive. The cell must not be published as a NSW project cost. Either restate it as an explicitly labelled half of the $7,600m whole-project amount or leave it unsourced. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#2 (blocker): Splitting the bundled $7,600m 'South West NSW REZ + Interconnector' amount into NSW and Victorian halves is an author decision with no source basis. The author must decide whether to publish a split at all, and on what stated rule; the pack records no NSW allocation. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#3 (warning): Attribution of the Dinawan works (500 kV switchyard, the two or three 500/330 kV transformers, and the Dinawan-end reactors brought forward from the Dinawan - Tragowel circuits) between the NSW component and the interconnector is not resolved by any archived source. The author must decide which Dinawan elements count as NSW component scope before any NSW-level count or cost is published. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp52-53; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p45]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#4 (warning): The NSW/Victoria lifecycle relationship is dual: AEMO states VNI West remains one actionable integrated ISP project and that integrated delivery is more efficient than staging, while categorising the South West NSW REZ component as an actionable NSW project under the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020 (NSW). The author must decide whether the chart treats VNI West (NSW) as a project in its own right or as a component of one project, since the two readings imply different rows. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp51-52]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#5 (warning): The hubs cell (1) rests on an author definition: no source describes the Dinawan 500 kV switchyard, or any other NSW element, as a hosting hub. Publish the hub count only with a stated author definition, or leave the cell blank. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#6 (warning): The alt_routes cell (0) rests on an author definition. AEMO 2020 assessed Shepparton and Kerang route alternatives and the current alignment was selected as option 5A in May 2023. The author must state whether historical or superseded alternatives count before publishing a zero. [HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3 pp33-36; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p51]
- CONTRADICTION#7 (warning): The chart states 6,000 MVA of transformers. AEMO 2026 specifies three 500/330 kV 1,500 MVA units at Dinawan and says the $7,600m includes two transformers only; AEMO 2024 specified two such units. No archived configuration reaches 6,000 MVA at the stated unit rating; the cell should be corrected or its basis stated. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p45]
- NON_ADDITIVE_BOUNDARIES#8 (warning): The $116m third-transformer increment is modelled separately above the $7,600m amount, which includes two transformers only; the $7,600m itself bundles the South West NSW REZ with the interconnector and contains $565m of approved early works and other incurred costs excluded from the ISP CBA. Do not form $7,716m, do not net out the $565m, and do not treat any portion as a NSW allocation. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52 and footnotes 103, 106]
- NON_COMPARABLE_SERIES#9 (warning): The 2020 cost ranges, the 2024 $3,499m (2020-21 dollars, inclusive of WRL) and $3,614m (real 2023 dollars, WRL removed), and the 2026 $7,600m ($2025, Class 4) differ in route, scope, dollar year and maturity. Do not build a revision series or compute growth between them; each is a discrete dated observation. [HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3 pp33-36; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p45; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p52]

Cross-family double counting is possible against the Western Renewables Link row; boundaries must be stated wherever these figures appear. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 pp44-45; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p53]

Circuit, line and km counts attributed to VNI West (NSW) overlap physical assets delivered and costed by other project families. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp52-53]
