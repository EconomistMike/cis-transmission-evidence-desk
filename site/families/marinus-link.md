# Project Marinus

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** Project Marinus includes Marinus Link and NWTD (2025 RIT-T pp6,18). The $5,035m Stage 1 and $2,535m Stage 2 figures, in June 2023 prices, are Project Marinus study-period costs, not Link-only construction outturn; the valuation period is shorter than project life and depends on cable 2 timing. AEMO 2026 p59 reports $7,570m in the feedback-loop context. Stage 2 Option 2A differs from Option 2: the source removes $184m Burnie-Hampshire Hills scope transferred to T2 Option 1, giving $2,351m in $2023 and $2,485m in $2025. No like-for-like Link-only replacement for the chart's $5.5bn is established; do not derive one by subtraction. Funding is not capex.

**Capacity.** Two staged 750 MW monopole HVDC links (1,500 MW total), operating alongside Basslink.

**Completion date.**

**Current status.** Stage 1 anticipated following the reported 1 August 2025 government FID; Stage 2 actionable and reassessed.

**Delivery status.** Stage 1 anticipated project (AEMO 2026 ISP); Stage 2 reassessed as an actionable ISP project (TAS-SEV Option 2A). No construction outturn captured.

**Dependent projects.** Tasmania REZ Expansion (T2 Option 1) builds the Burnie-Hampshire Hills 220 kV lines removed from Stage 2 Option 2A; North West Transmission Developments (TasNetworks); existing Basslink interconnector operates in parallel.

**Funding pathway.** Actionable ISP project under the RIT-T, feedback-loop and contingent-project framework. AEMO reports a Tasmanian, Victorian and Australian government Final Investment Decision for Stage 1 on 1 August 2025; the operative decision or funding instrument is not archived.

**Identity.** Project Marinus comprises Marinus Link (HVDC interconnector) and TasNetworks NWTD (HVAC network works); Stage 1 and Stage 2 and Option 2/2A are distinct scope/version boundaries.

**Name.** Project Marinus

**Owner.** Marinus Link Pty Ltd delivers the HVDC interconnector; TasNetworks delivers the North West Transmission Developments (NWTD) HVAC works. AEMO reports both collectively as Project Marinus.

**Schedule.** Stage 1: December 2030. Stage 2 proponent timing: December 2034.

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP", "HUM-AEMO-2022-ISP", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "ML-TN-2021-PACR", "ML-EY-2024-RITT", "ML-TN-2025-RITT", "ML-EY-2025-ADD", "ML-DCCEEW-2025-FID", "ML-MLPL-2025-CONTRACT"]
