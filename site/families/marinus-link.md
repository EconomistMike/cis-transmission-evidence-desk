# Project Marinus

## Overview

ML-PROJECT-MARINUS | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 10 sources | 32 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One provisional Project Marinus; Stage 1 and Stage 2 are separate components/versions pending lifecycle review.

**Current status.** Stage 1 anticipated following the reported 1 August 2025 government FID; Stage 2 actionable and reassessed.

**Capacity.** Two staged 750 MW monopole HVDC links (1,500 MW total), operating alongside Basslink.

**Schedule.** Stage 1: December 2030. Stage 2 proponent timing: December 2034.

**Owner.** Marinus Link Pty Ltd delivers the HVDC interconnector; TasNetworks delivers the North West Transmission Developments (NWTD) HVAC works. AEMO reports both collectively as Project Marinus.

**Funding pathway.** Actionable ISP project under the RIT-T, feedback-loop and contingent-project framework. AEMO reports a Tasmanian, Victorian and Australian government Final Investment Decision for Stage 1 on 1 August 2025; the operative decision or funding instrument is not archived.

**Delivery status.** Stage 1 anticipated project (AEMO 2026 ISP); Stage 2 reassessed as an actionable ISP project (TAS-SEV Option 2A). No construction outturn captured.

**Dependent projects.** Tasmania REZ Expansion (T2 Option 1) builds the Burnie-Hampshire Hills 220 kV lines removed from Stage 2 Option 2A; North West Transmission Developments (TasNetworks); existing Basslink interconnector operates in parallel.

## Warnings and open decisions

- CONTRADICTION#3 (blocker): The chart row states $5.5 billion and 1.4 GW. No archived source states either value for any Project Marinus boundary; sources state 1,500 MW and, for the chart's own two-stage boundary, a reported total of $7,570 million in $2023. The cost and capacity cells should not be published as they stand. Publish the source-defined observations with their boundaries, or mark the cells as under review. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; ML-TN-2025-RITT; ML-TN-2021-PACR; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp26, 58-59; ML-TN-2025-RITT p18; ML-TN-2021-PACR p77]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#11 (warning): Three definitions are the author's to settle: whether any inclusive cost view (Project Marinus plus NWTD) may be published; whether an HVDC cable route counts as trunk kilometres when the chart defines trunk as distance rated at 500 kV; and how alt routes and hubs are counted for a subsea interconnector. Until the author decides, the cost cell stays at the source-defined boundary, the trunk cell stays marked as derived, and alt_routes and hubs stay marked as judgments. [Policy Paper Chart notes rows 16-22; ML-TN-2025-RITT p6; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp26-27]
- NON_COMPARABLE_SERIES#1 (warning): The 2021, 2024, 2025 and 2026 amounts differ in scope, price basis, maturity and methodology. Do not calculate an automatic earliest-to-latest percentage or label it an overrun. [ML-TN-2021-PACR; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; ML-TN-2025-RITT; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; ML-TN-2021-PACR pp16-17, 85; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p37; ML-TN-2025-RITT p18; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p59]
- NON_ADDITIVE_BOUNDARIES#2 (warning): HVDC, HVAC/NWTD, Stage 1, Stage 2 and transferred REZ works have distinct boundaries. Do not automatically sum separately stated values. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; ML-TN-2025-RITT; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p37; ML-TN-2025-RITT p18; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp58-59]
- PRICE_BASIS_UNKNOWN#4 (warning): The 2024 ISP Appendix 5 project table states expected costs for Project Marinus without naming a dollar year, so ML-AO-2024-* observations carry a null price basis. Do not compare the 2024 ISP amounts with the $2020, $2023 or $2025 figures, and do not assume a dollar year for them. [HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5 p37]
- SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE#5 (warning): The joint ministerial media release announcing Stage 1 financial close (3 September 2025) is now archived as ML-DCCEEW-2025-FID and is the archived record of the decision. The operative FID/funding instrument itself (RECFiT decision, CEFC finance agreement, or equivalent) remains unavailable through two official routes (RECFiT decision page HTTP 403; AEMO feedback-loop announcement HTTP 403). The announcement is captured but not the instrument; treat the $3.8bn CEFC funding figure and equity commitments as reported by the announcement, not independently verified against the underlying legal/financial instrument. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; ML-DCCEEW-2025-FID; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp26-27; ML-DCCEEW-2025-FID web; discovery record ML-DISC-2025-FID]
- SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE#6 (warning): The underlying 2024 proponent RIT-T update returned HTTP 404 at two attempted attachment routes. The EY report is retained as modelling evidence, not substituted as the proponent cost record. [ML-EY-2024-RITT; discovery record ML-DISC-2024-RITT]
- OUTTURN_UNKNOWN#7 (warning): A Stage 1 civils and construction contract value is now archived ($994 million nominal, TasVic Greenlink, executed 17 December 2025), but no current construction outturn (actual spend to date) has been captured. Treat the $994m figure as an executed contract price, not a construction outturn; all other monetary values remain estimates or planner/proponent observations. [ML-TN-2025-RITT; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; ML-MLPL-2025-CONTRACT; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp26-27, 58-59; ML-MLPL-2025-CONTRACT web; research search gap]
- SCOPE_TRANSFER#8 (warning): The 2026 $2,485m Stage 2 option excludes $184m Burnie-Hampshire scope transferred to Tasmania REZ Expansion. It is not directly comparable with the $2,535m Stage 2 observation. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p59]
- SHARED_ATTRIBUTION#9 (warning): Tasmania REZ Expansion (T2 Option 1) and the NWTD works share scope and benefits with Project Marinus but are separate actionable projects with their own cost boundaries. Do not allocate Tasmania REZ Expansion or NWTD amounts to the Marinus chart row, and do not treat the row as covering all Tasmanian works enabled by the interconnector. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; ML-TN-2025-RITT; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 pp26, 58-59; ML-TN-2025-RITT p18]
- CHART_DERIVATION#10 (warning): The chart's 345 km trunk length is the sum of two separately stated approximate HVDC cable lengths (255 km subsea, 90 km underground), and Millions/trunk KM is a workbook formula over that sum and the contradicted cost cell. Neither derived cell is an archived figure. Publish the two length observations separately, and do not publish a cost per kilometre. [ML-TN-2025-RITT; ML-TN-2025-RITT p6]
