# Project EnergyConnect

Research cutoff: 2026-09-04. Archived-evidence audit correction: 2026-09-07 (not new research).

## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** There is no archived reconciled whole-project cost for Project EnergyConnect. The AER approved two separate owner allowances, $457.4m ($2017-18) for ElectraNet and $1,817.9m ($2017-18) for TransGrid, and described the project as a proposed $2.3 billion ($2017-18) contingent project. TransGrid's February 2026 reopener application, citing Contract Failure, now states a forecast cost of $3,628 million (real 2022-23) to complete TransGrid's own NSW component - a proponent forecast, not an AER-approved amount, and not stated to include ElectraNet's SA works. The chart's $4.1 billion still matches only Transgrid's separate social-value claim, a benefit measure, and the chart's 160 km now has an exact but different match: Transgrid's June 2026 completion release states Stage 1 (SA border to Buronga and into Victoria) is 160 km, distinct from the 165 km Dinawan-Wagga Wagga section and from the 900 km whole-route figure both Transgrid's page and the joint ElectraNet/Transgrid project page state. Cost, capacity, trunk km, syncons and transformers require the stated boundary corrections; line and feeder interpretations depend on explicit chart definitions.

**Capacity.** Stage 1 delivered 150 MW in both directions between NSW and SA. AEMO expects the NSW-SA interconnector to increase to 800 MW in both directions after Stage 2, with a further 100 MW VIC-SA. No archived source states 2.2 GW.

**Completion date.** Stage 1: late 2024 (AEMO 2026), though Transgrid's June 2026 completion release instead says Stage 1 'became operational last year' (i.e. 2025) - a contradiction, not resolved. Stage 2: November 2027 (AEMO 2026), against Transgrid's stated NEM integration by end of 2026; Transgrid's June 2026 release reports the 700 km NSW section construction complete and Stage 2 being energised ahead of inter-network testing

**Current status.** Committed and under construction. AEMO's 2026 ISP records Stage 1 (Robertstown via Bundey to Buronga) completed in late 2024 and Stage 2 committed for November 2027; Transgrid's own page states construction and initial energisation are complete with NEM integration due by the end of 2026. Transgrid's June 2026 completion release states construction of the 700 km NSW section is complete and Stage 2 (the 540 km Buronga-Wagga Wagga line) is being energised ahead of AEMO's inter-network testing; it also states Stage 1 (160 km, SA border to Buronga and into Victoria) 'became operational last year', which is not consistent with AEMO's 'late 2024' Stage 1 completion date. Transgrid's February 2026 application to reopen its 2023-2028 revenue determination cites Contract Failure (recognised by Transgrid's Board on 25 October 2023) and a resulting forecast cost increase to $3,628 million (real 2022-23) to complete the NSW component; this reopener had not been decided by the AER as at the 2026-09-03 research cutoff.

**Delivery status.** Stage 1 complete (late 2024); Stage 2 under construction and committed

**Dependent projects.** Interfaces with HumeLink and VNI West at Wagga Wagga and Dinawan; these are dependencies, not PEC components. The Victorian connection is a 220 kV spur at Red Cliffs.

**Funding pathway.** AER contingent project determinations under the 2018-23 regulatory control periods, one per owner (both final 28 May 2021)

**Identity.** One Project EnergyConnect delivered jointly by ElectraNet (South Australia) and TransGrid (New South Wales), with a 220 kV spur to Victoria at Red Cliffs. Stage 1 and Stage 2 are components of one project; the SA and NSW owner boundaries are separate regulatory proceedings, not separate projects.

**Name.** Project EnergyConnect

**Owner.** ElectraNet (South Australia) and TransGrid (New South Wales), jointly constructing and operating

**Schedule.** 2020 ISP: staged commissioning late 2022 to late 2023. 2022 ISP: July 2026. 2024 ISP: Stage 1 December 2024, Stage 2 July 2027. 2026 ISP: Stage 1 completed late 2024, Stage 2 November 2027.

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2020-ISP-A3", "HUM-AEMO-2022-ISP", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "PEC-AER-2021-ELECTRANET-DECISION", "PEC-AER-2021-TRANSGRID-DECISION", "PEC-AER-2020-TRANSGRID-APPLICATION", "PEC-AER-2020-ELECTRANET-APPLICATION", "PEC-TG-PROJECT-PAGE", "PEC-AER-2026-REOPEN", "PEC-TG-2026-ATTACHMENT2", "PEC-TG-2026-COMPLETION", "PEC-JOINT-2026-PROJECT-PAGE"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 330 kV interconnector; the Robertstown-Bundey section is 275 kV and the Dinawan-Wagga Wagga section is built at 500 kV but operated at 330 kV
