# Sydney Ring South (Reinforcing Sydney, Newcastle and Wollongong Supply)

## Overview

SRS-PROJECT-SRS | Research cut-off: 2026-09-04 | 5 sources | 49 observations

## Identity and status

**Identity.** One actionable ISP project containing two AEMO-identified options: power flow control (CNSW-SNW Option 2d) and Bannaby – South Creek 500 kV transmission (CNSW-SNW Option 3). Transgrid PADR Option 6 is a combined staged version of the same project, not a second project.

**Current status.** Actionable ISP project in the 2024 and 2026 ISPs; Transgrid's PADR is under consultation. No approval, feedback-loop confirmation or final decision is archived.

**Capacity.** 3,600 MW additional forward capacity from the 500 kV option; 0 MW from the power flow control option. Existing CNSW-SNW South flow path is modelled at 2,540 MW (peak/summer typical) and 2,720 MW (winter).

**Schedule.** Proponent timing July 2030 (power flow control) and July 2033 (500 kV line); 2026 ISP ODP delivers both stages in 2033-34; the PADR places the South Creek 500/330 kV substation in 2030-31.

**Owner.** Transgrid (RIT-T proponent)

**Funding pathway.** National Electricity Rules ISP framework and RIT-T; contingent project application to the AER would follow an AEMO feedback-loop confirmation. Not archived: no CPA or determination.

**Delivery status.** Draft RIT-T stage (PADR under consultation); early works described as engagement, approvals, land, procurement and project development.

**Dependent projects.** Hunter Transmission Project (CNSW-SNW Option 1 / Sydney Ring North) is a stated prerequisite for the power flow control option and is a separate project under the NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020.

## Warnings and open decisions

- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#3 (blocker): Two archived cost figures exist for this row on different bases: the AEMO-modelled $2,621m ($2025, both options combined) and Transgrid's own PADR Option 6 total of $3,519m (nominal, staged $1,135m then $2,384m). The chart's $3.510bn matches neither basis and is a transcription of $3,519m. The author must choose which basis the chart publishes ($2025 modelled or nominal PADR) and correct the transcription; the pack neither converts between price bases nor treats the difference as growth. [HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5; SRS-TG-2026-PADR; HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5 p43 (footnote 85); SRS-TG-2026-PADR p38]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#5 (warning): The chart records 1 alternative route. No archived source counts alternative routes; the claim rests on the author's reading of PADR Figure 2.5 and on geographic diversity through the Sydney Ring. alt_routes is an author judgment. Shared South Creek and Sydney West interfaces and rebuilds of existing lines do not establish independent route security. [Policy Paper table notes, Sydney Ring South, Alt Routes; research/sydney-ring-south-source-chain.md]
- AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED#6 (warning): The chart's 114 km trunk length is now archived, matching Transgrid's 2023 preparatory-activities estimate for the Bannaby-South Creek line exactly, but Transgrid's later 2026 PADR instead describes the (differently scoped) Bannaby-South Western Sydney line as extending to around 100 km. The author must decide which vintage and boundary the trunk_km cell represents: the 2023 preparatory estimate (114 km, matches the chart) or the current 2026 PADR estimate (around 100 km). [SRS-TG-2023-SOUTHERN-RING; SRS-TG-2026-PADR; SRS-TG-2023-SOUTHERN-RING p8; SRS-TG-2026-PADR p43]
