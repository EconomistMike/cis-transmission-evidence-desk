## Identity and status

**Analysis conclusion.** The immutable chart contains $3.5bn, consistent with rounding the PADR Option 6 total of $3,519m nominal to one decimal billion. The source gives Stage 1 $1,135m and Stage 2 $2,384m, P50 Class 5 (+100%/-50%). AEMO's $2,621m modelled $2025 amount is a different price basis. No transcription error is established (PADR p38; AEMO 2026 p43). 30.70175438596491 = 3,500 AUD million / 114 km, using the original chart inputs. The $3.5bn rounds the $3,519m nominal Option 6 estimate; the 114 km is the older 2023 preparatory route, whereas the 2026 PADR describes approximately 100 km on a revised scope. This mixed-vintage ratio is not a reconciled source-defined construction cost per km (2023 preparatory p8; PADR pp38,43).

**Capacity.** 3,600 MW additional forward capacity from the 500 kV option; 0 MW from the power flow control option. Existing CNSW-SNW South flow path is modelled at 2,540 MW (peak/summer typical) and 2,720 MW (winter).

**Completion date.** 2033-34 financial year (2026 ISP ODP)

**Current status.** Actionable ISP project in the 2024 and 2026 ISPs; Transgrid's PADR is under consultation. No approval, feedback-loop confirmation or final decision is archived.

**Delivery status.** Draft RIT-T stage (PADR under consultation); early works described as engagement, approvals, land, procurement and project development.

**Dependent projects.** Hunter Transmission Project (CNSW-SNW Option 1 / Sydney Ring North) is a stated prerequisite for the power flow control option and is a separate project under the NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act 2020.

**Funding pathway.** National Electricity Rules ISP framework and RIT-T; contingent project application to the AER would follow an AEMO feedback-loop confirmation. Not archived: no CPA or determination.

**Identity.** One actionable ISP project containing two AEMO-identified options: power flow control (CNSW-SNW Option 2d) and Bannaby – South Creek 500 kV transmission (CNSW-SNW Option 3). Transgrid PADR Option 6 is a combined staged version of the same project, not a second project.

**Name.** Sydney Ring South (Reinforcing Sydney, Newcastle and Wollongong Supply)

**Owner.** Transgrid (RIT-T proponent)

**Schedule.** Proponent timing July 2030 (power flow control) and July 2033 (500 kV line); 2026 ISP ODP delivers both stages in 2033-34; the PADR places the South Creek 500/330 kV substation in 2030-31.

**Source ids.** ["HUM-AEMO-2026-ISP-A5", "HUM-AEMO-2024-ISP-A5", "SRS-TG-2023-SOUTHERN-RING", "SRS-TG-2026-PADR", "SRS-TG-2026-PROJECTPAGE"]

**Trunk voltage kv.** 330 kV line built to a 500 kV design; South Creek upgraded to a 330/500 kV substation
