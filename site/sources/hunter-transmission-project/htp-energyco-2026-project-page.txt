 Hunter Transmission Project

 Key details
 As coal-fired power stations close, existing transmission lines lack the capacity needed to transfer electricity from renewable energy zones (REZs) to consumers.  
The Hunter Transmission Project involves building a new, 110-kilometre, overhead 500 kilovolt (kV) transmission line between Bayswater in the Upper Hunter and Olney in the Lower Hunter. It will connect the state’s existing 500 kV transmission lines, which will create a 500 kV ring of transmission infrastructure that will provide the backbone of the state’s new electricity grid for generations to come. 
Importantly, it will unlock the electricity supply from the Central-West Orana and New England REZs.

 Planning approval granted for the Hunter Transmission Project | July 2026
 The NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure has granted planning approval for the Hunter Transmission Project, with the Minister for Planning and Public Spaces issuing the Conditions of Approval following a detailed and independent assessment process.
The project team is now progressing detailed design and planning activities in line with the Conditions of Approval.
Before construction can begin, the team must complete detailed design, management plans and all pre-construction requirements in accordance with the Conditions of Approval.
As the project moves through detailed design, planning and delivery preparation, the project team will continue engaging with landholders, communities and stakeholders.
