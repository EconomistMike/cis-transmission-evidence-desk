Central West Orana Renewable Energy Zone - Transfer of Barigan Creek from ACEREZ Partnership to Transgrid 
I am writing to advise that on 15 October 2024, the ASL Board, in its capacity as the Consumer Trustee, approved the transfer of 
the Barigan Creek Switching Station from ACEREZ Partnership to Transgrid in accordance with clause 21(2) of the Electricity 
Infrastructure Investment Regulation 2021 (EII Regulation) and the Notices of Authorisation (NoAs) for the Central-West Orana 
Main and Enabling REZ Network Infrastructure Projects (CWO RNIPs) dated 4 June 2024. 
Consistent with the NOAs, the ASL Board’s approval means that the Barigan Creek Switching Station will no longer be authorised 
under the NoA for the Main CWO RNIP, as ACEREZ Partnership will cease to be its authorised network operator. Instead, this 
network infrastructure will be deemed authorised under the NoA for the Enabling CWO RNIP, with Transgrid becoming the 
authorised network operator for the Barigan Creek Switching Station. 
Thank you for providing us with a draft copy of the public summary of your recommendation for the transfer. We are currently 
reviewing the summary and will share any comments by Wednesday 29 October, so you may publish it on the EnergyCo 
website, which we understand is planned for Friday 7 November. We plan to update the ASL website on Tuesday, 11 November, 
to publish the Consumer Trustee’s decision approving the transfer. This update will include a brief description of the transfer, as 
outlined above, and a link to your public summary.
