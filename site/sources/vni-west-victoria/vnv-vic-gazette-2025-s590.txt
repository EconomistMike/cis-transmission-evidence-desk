National Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005
SPECIAL ORDER AMENDING THE VNI WEST AND WRL MINISTERIAL ORDER 
DATED 20 FEBRUARY 2023
I, Lily D’Ambrosio, Minister for Energy and Resources and Minister responsible for administering 
the National Electricity (Victoria) Act 2005 (the Act), make the following Special Order pursuant 
to section 16ZHA of the Act.
PRELIMINARY
1. Purpose
The purpose of this Special Order is to amend the Order made under section 16Y of the Act 
and published in the Government Gazette No. S 60 on 20 February 2023 (16Y Order).
2. Commencement
This Special Order commences on 1 November 2025.
3. Amendments
3.1 In clause 2 of the 16Y Order: 
(a) in the definition of ‘AEMO’s costs’, substitute all references to ‘AEMO’ with 
‘VicGrid’; and
(b) for ‘AEMO’s costs’ substitute ‘VicGrid’s costs’.
3.2 In clause 2 of the 16Y Order, after the definition of ‘augmentation’, insert:
‘Board means the board of directors of VicGrid Body Corporate established by the 
Governor in Council under section 14(1) of the State Owned Enterprises Act 1992.’ 
3.3 For the definition of VicGrid in clause 2 of the 16Y Order, substitute:
‘VicGrid means the VicGrid Body Corporate established by the Governor in Council 
under section 14(1) of the State Owned Enterprises Act 1992.’
3.4 In clause 2 of the 16Y Order, the definition of ‘ VNI West construction agreement ’ 
is deleted.
3.5 In the definition of VNI West RIT-T in clause 2 of the 16Y Order, delete ‘being’.
3.6 In the definition of WRL PACR in clause 2 of the 16Y Order, delete ‘by AEMO’.
3.7 In the definition of WRL Project Document in clause 2 of the 16Y Order, substitute 
the reference to ‘AEMO’ with ‘VicGrid’.

3.8 In clause 4 of the 16Y Order, delete ‘by AEMO’.
3.9 In the heading ‘AEMO’S FUNCTIONS’, substitute the reference to ‘AEMO’ with 
‘VicGrid’.
3.10 In clauses 5, 6, 7 and 8 of the 16Y Order,  substitute all references to ‘AEMO’ with 
‘VicGrid’.
3.11 In Note 1 and Note 2 under clause 5.1 of the 16Y Order, substitute all references  
to ‘AEMO’ with ‘VicGrid’.
3.12 Clauses 6.1(d), 6.1(g), 6.3 and 8.4(b) of the 16Y Order are revoked.
3.13 In clause 6.2(a) of the 16Y Order, substitute the reference to ‘the Board of AEMO’ 
with ‘the Board’.
4. Savings
4.1 Nothing in this Special Order:
(a) affects the validity of any act taken or any function exercised under the 16Y 
Order prior to the making of this Special Order; or
(b) prevents a person or body from recovering any costs incurred prior to the 
making of this Special Order, which it would have been entitled to recover 
under the 16Y Order if this Special Order had not been made. 
Dated 15 October 2025
