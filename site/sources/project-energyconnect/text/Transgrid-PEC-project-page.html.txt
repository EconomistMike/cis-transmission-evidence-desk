                                    <h2>Building a reliable clean energy future</h2>
                                </div>
                                <div class="rich-content">
                                    <p>Reliable electricity is what keeps our homes, hospitals and businesses running every day. With EnergyConnect, we’re taking a big step towards securing that reliability, while opening the way for more renewable energy.</p>
<p>Construction and initial energisation is complete for EnergyConnect <!-- x-tinymce/html -->– Australia’s largest energy transmission project. <span>Asset commissioning works are progressing ahead of inter-networking testing and integration into the National Electricity Market (NEM) by the end of 2026. </span></p>
<p>Stretching 900km across New South Wales, Victoria and South Australia, it has connected the three state grids for the very first time. That means:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Greater security of supply</strong> – keeping communities powered, even as energy demand changes.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Room for renewables</strong> – creating pathways for new wind, solar and other clean energy sources to plug into the grid as coal-fired plants close.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>A stronger national network</strong> – enabling states to share energy when and where it’s needed most.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>As a <a rel="noopener" href="https://pp.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/development-and-assessment/state-significant-infrastructure" target="_blank">Critical State Significant Infrastructure (CSSI)</a> project, EnergyConnect is central to the <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/major-publications/integrated-system-plan-isp" target="_blank">Australian Energy Market Operator’s plan</a> for a more sustainable energy system.</p>
<p><span>Over the life of the project, Transgrid and our delivery partner have </span>worked closely with communities, landholders and stakeholders to carefully plan and manage impacts to the surrounding areas. EnergyConnect isn't just new infrastructure, it’s a foundation for a cleaner, more reliable energy future.</p>
